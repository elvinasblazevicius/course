## The book (draft)
A **large-print cozy (non-gory) village murder mystery told through word searches**, for adults 55+ and cozy-mystery readers.
- **Story:** one continuous case. Each puzzle opens with a short scene (a witness statement, a note, an alibi) and hides a word list of names, places and clues.
- **Mechanism:** each solved grid gives one real story clue plus one leftover hidden letter. Collected, the letters spell the answer to the final reveal: who did it, how and why. The puzzles feed the plot, which is the gap readers name in the comps.
- **Verification by code:** every word placed and findable exactly once; the leftover-letter message is exact; every clue needed for the solution is present, with no contradictions (a logic checker over the case facts).
- **Large print:** 8.5x11, grids about 15x15 with letters of 16pt or more, high-contrast black, solutions with the hidden words outlined.
- **Series:** volume 1 non-dated "Winter" (sells past Christmas), then seasonal and setting volumes. It cross-sells with KDP-000071 *Large Print Word Search for Adults and Seniors*.
- **Naming:** own coined series name; no "Murd-", "-doku", Clue/Cluedo, Hunt A Killer, or "The Killer Isn't [Name]" pattern.
