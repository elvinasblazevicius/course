100 Tips for Successful Dating on Dating Apps ELENA MARLOWE VALE
Every dating app book tells you to “just be yourself” and “stay positive.” Neither one tells you what to actually write in a bio, how to tell a real match from a time-waster in the first three messages, or when to stop swiping and go outside instead.
This book is the practical half. One hundred concrete moves for the profile, the opening message, the in-app conversation, and the leap to a real first date – built for the apps as they actually work, not as the marketing copy describes them.
INSIDE, AMONG THE HUNDRED
  • A profile and photo set that actually gets matches, not just views
  • Opening lines that get replies, and the ones that kill a conversation
  • Reading red flags in a bio and in the first few messages
  • Getting from chatting to a real date without it fizzling out
  • Staying safe: verification, video calls, and the first meetup
  • Handling ghosting, rejection, and matches that go nowhere
  • Avoiding burnout and swipe fatigue over a long search
  • Knowing when an app conversation is worth your time at all
One hundred concrete actions for dating apps, each ending with one thing to do today. No generic positivity, and no pretending every match deserves your time.
